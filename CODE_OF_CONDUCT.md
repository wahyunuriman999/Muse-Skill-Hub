# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Muse Skill Hub a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Claiming someone else's work as your own

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to **wahyunuriman999@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed
and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer is obligated to respect
the privacy and security of the reporter.

Enforcement actions may include warnings, temporary bans, or permanent
expulsion from the project, at the maintainer's discretion.
